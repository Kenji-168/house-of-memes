About the Collection

The House of Memes is a small virtual museum for the internet's most recognizable visual jokes. From the surreal to the sincere, these images document how online humor travels, mutates, and becomes a shared language across platforms.

The collection is curated for curious visitors who want a few excellent specimens from the history of meme culture, presented like a respectful gallery catalog with a wink.
